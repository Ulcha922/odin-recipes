Shakshuka

Shakshuka is a dish which is perfect for breakfast!

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 bell-pepper
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 80 ml tomato paste
  • Cheese
  • Herbs
  • Pepper and salt

Steps

  1. Chop onion in smaller pieces and fry for 1 minute on the pan.
  2. Add bell-pepper, tomato and tomato-paste to the onion.
  3. Stew on the middle heat 5-7 minutes, add salt and spices.
  4. Make two deepenings in the vegetables and add egges.
  5. Cook until the egg-whites are ready.
  6. Add cheese and herbs. Your shakshuka is ready!
